# Shopping list (Amazon.ca)

Everything needed for the scan station, feeder and the scan bed + chute, beyond the Bambu
Lab A1. Search Amazon.ca for the **bold** words. Prices are rough, in CAD, and change often.

## The brain and camera

- [ ] **Raspberry Pi 5 4GB starter kit** (comes with the 27 W power supply, a case with a fan,
      and a microSD card). CanaKit's kits are a safe pick. About $150.
- [ ] **Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3**, the standard one, not "Wide" or "NoIR". About $35.
- [ ] **Raspberry Pi 5 camera cable 300 mm** (22-pin to 15-pin). The cable that comes with
      the camera doesn't fit the Pi 5. About $10.

## Moving parts

- [ ] **MG90S micro servo, 4-pack** (metal gear). One for the stop pins, one to tilt the
      chute, two for the trapdoors. About $20.
- [ ] **PCA9685 16-channel servo driver board**. Lets the Pi run all the servos smoothly. About $12.
- [ ] **N20 gear motor 6V 150 RPM** (3 mm D-shaft). Turns the feeder roller. Often sold in
      2-packs: get a spare. About $15.
- [ ] **DRV8833 motor driver** (usually a pack of a few). Lets the Pi switch the feeder motor
      on and off. About $10.

## Power and light

- [ ] **5V 4A power adapter** with a 5.5 × 2.1 mm barrel plug, plus **DC barrel jack screw
      terminal adapters**. Powers the servos and LEDs separately from the Pi, so they don't
      make it crash. About $20.
- [ ] **5V USB LED strip, cool white / daylight (6000K), 8–10 mm wide**. Not warm white:
      yellow light makes cards harder to recognize. About $15.

## Small hardware

- [ ] **Metric O-ring assortment, nitrile**. Make sure it includes **20 mm ID × 3 mm**
      (that's the feeder roller's tyre). About $15.
- [ ] **M2 M3 self-tapping screw assortment**. Servos, camera, brackets, hinge pins. About $15.
- [ ] **Dupont jumper wire kit** (male-female and female-female). About $10.
- [ ] **Small zip ties**. About $5.

## Filament

You're getting 2 rolls of Bambu PLA Tough with the printer. On top of those:

- [ ] **White PLA, 1 kg**: the scan bed and scan station base (the camera's background).
- [ ] **Black PLA, 1 kg**, or 2 kg if you print the bins too: the frame, walls and bins.

About $25–30 per kg.

## Handy to have

- [ ] **Digital calipers**: for measuring parts and checking fits. About $20.
- [ ] **99% isopropyl alcohol**: wipe the A1's build plate so prints stick. About $10.
- [ ] **4×AA battery holder with switch**: test the feeder before the Pi is wired up. About $5.

**Total: roughly $400–450**, most of it the Pi kit and camera.
