Teach the scanner

It starts out knowing each card from one clean picture. Your own scans, with your camera and your lighting, teach it what cards really look like on your table.

  1. CollectTurn on Collect scans and scan like normal.
  2. ConfirmSaving a card confirms it. When it's wrong, press Wrong card? and pick the right one, or label scans below.
  3. TrainWith Keep learning on, it learns each card you confirm as soon as you take it away. Train now catches up on the rest.
