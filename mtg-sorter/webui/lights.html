Flash while scanning

The bars flash a color for a moment, then go back to the color above. If the bars light the scan bed, colored flashes tint the camera's picture: keep them outside the camera's view, or turn this off.

Setup

Count the LEDs on both bars together. Not sure? Lower the number until the last LED goes dark, then add one back. Too many does no harm; it only makes the power estimate too high.

Wiring
  1. The kit's motherboard plug (3-pin ARGB) has 5V, D (data) and G (ground), plus one empty spot. 5V is usually the side with the arrow; check the labels on the plug or cable.
  2. 5V and G go to your 5 V supply, on the same screw jack as the servos. Never 12 V: it burns the LEDs out at once.
  3. D goes to GPIO10, pin 19 on the Pi, with a dupont jumper pushed into the plug.
  4. The bars and the Pi have to share a ground. On the same 5 V supply they already do; on a separate one, also run a wire from G to the Pi's pin 20.
  5. SPI has to be on. Press Turn SPI on above if the page asks, then reboot.
  6. Flickering or random colors? Keep the data wire short, and put a 330 Ω resistor in it if you have one.

Full white is about 60 mA per LED, so 60 LEDs could pull 3.6 A. Your 5 V 4 A supply also runs the Pi, so the app dims the bars to stay under 1 A. Keep the brightness low unless you get a bigger supply.
