Accounts

Give everyone who uses the sorter their own login, with their own collection, decks, wishlist, trade binder and games, and pick what each person is allowed to do. You become the owner: an admin with every permission, and every card already here stays yours. A backup is saved first. The login replaces the PIN lock.

People and permissions

Everyone here has their own cards. Pick a role, or tick the exact permissions. Taking someone away keeps their cards on the server: add the same username again to bring them back.

Prices and card data

Once a week the Pi checks the Near Mint price of every card in your collection, decks, wishlist and trade binder at Face to Face Games (the main price), 401 Games, The Mythic Store, Hobbiesville, and the Montreal stores Card Brawlers and L'Univers de la Carte, and updates its list of every Commander card for Card lookup and the deck builder. Bring in Commander Vault

Commander Vault is part of this app now. Pick the export file from Commander Vault and everything comes in: each collection source (as its own source on the Collection page), your decks with their guides, your wishlist, your trade binder, games and the latest Canadian prices. Anything already brought in is skipped, so it's safe to do twice.

Alerts while scanning

The scanner page chimes and shows a banner for these, on every phone and laptop.

PIN lock

Checking…

With a PIN, every page asks for it before anyone can see or change anything, so other people on your Wi-Fi can't. Each phone or laptop stays unlocked for 30 days after typing it. The Pi itself is never locked out. Forgot it? On the Pi, delete data/pin.json.

Look

Theme (dark, light or black), accent and bin colors, font, spacing and what the scanner shows. It's kept in each browser, so your phone and laptop can each have their own.
