Servos

Mault sorter: its angles start as guesses. Each module has a trapdoor (door), a paddle and a pusher. Set each servo with its slider, then save it under the position it is in. Bin 1 and 2 are module 1's left and right, bin 3 and 4 module 2's, bin 5 the end bin. The feed buttons turn the feeder servo on channel 15. Sort runs come once the machine is built.

Mk2: its angles start as guesses. Set each servo before you trust Home: drag its slider slowly until the part sits right, then press the position under it to save. Stop bar up holds the card, down lets it go. Each pusher's centre is the paddle in the middle, left and right the two ends. Each trapdoor's closed is level and shut, open is hanging down.

The buttons go to each part's saved angles. To change one, move the slider until the part sits right, then press that position under Save the slider's angle as. It's kept on the Pi, so there's no file to edit. Centre each servo at 90 before pressing its horn on.

Feeder

Feed one card runs the motor at the speed above while watching the Pi camera. When a card lands just above the stop teeth it keeps pushing for Push after landing so the card's tail clears the gate, stops, then turns the wheels backwards for Back-off so the next card, which followed it out of the slot, goes back into the hopper (longer = pushed further back; too long drags the landed card back too). It gives up after 3 seconds, or sooner when a card stops moving half out of the slot (the gate is too tight). Start with the bed empty. Save as the usual push keeps all four numbers; Start sorting uses them. Timed push runs the motor for the seconds above, whatever happens.

Feed and sort one card to

Bin 1-4 on Mk2 feeds a card as above, drops the stop bar so the card lands on level 1, and puts the stop bar back up. Bin 1 and 2: level 1's pusher slides it off to the left or right. Bin 3 and 4: both trapdoors open, the card falls to level 2, the doors shut, and level 2's pusher slides it left or right.

Start sorting

On Mk2, set every servo's angles above and try each Bin button with a card before you start a run.

The machine on its own: it feeds a card, the scanner recognizes it, it is saved to the collection and dropped into the bin your Bins rules give it, then the next one, until the hopper is empty or you press Stop. The stop pins only drop for a card that is recognized: one the scanner isn't sure of, or can't read in time, stays on the bed with the pins up until you choose Look again, Unread bin (not saved, dropped into the fallback bin) or Stop. If the scanner reads it meanwhile, it carries on by itself. Keep your hands out while it runs.

Test run

Feeds a card and drops it, bin 1, 2, 3, 4 in turn, without reading it, for the number of cards you pick. It stops at the first problem (nothing came out, a card over the stop bar, a hand or two cards on the bed, a card left behind) and says why. Keep your hands out while it runs.

Tuning run

A mechanical test, not sorting: it feeds and drops each card straight away without reading it. Sorts card after card round the bins and scores every feed. With adjust on it slows the feeder after a card goes over the teeth, speeds it up when nothing comes, and keeps the pins down longer when a card needed a second drop. It stops if a card is left on the bed or the hopper runs out. Keep your hands out while it runs. Nothing is saved until you press Save.
