Machine

Servos

The buttons go to each part's saved angles. To change one, move the slider until the part sits right, then press that position under Save the slider's angle as. It's kept on the Pi, so there's no file to edit. Centre each servo at 90 before pressing its horn on.

Feeder

Feed one card runs the motor at the speed above while watching the Pi camera, and stops the moment a card lands just above the stop teeth (or after 3 seconds), then turns the wheels backwards for a moment so the next card, which followed it out of the slot, goes back into the hopper. Start with the bed empty. Timed push runs it for the seconds above, whatever happens.

Feed and sort one card to

Bin 1/2/3 feeds a card as above, tilts the chute to that bin, drops the stop pins for 3 seconds (a card takes a moment to slide), opens the trapdoors for bin 2, then puts everything back. If the camera still sees the card on the bed, it drops it once more.

Tuning run

Sorts card after card round the bins and scores every feed. With adjust on it slows the feeder after a card goes over the teeth, speeds it up when nothing comes, and keeps the pins down longer when a card needed a second drop. It stops if a card is left on the bed or the hopper runs out. Keep your hands out while it runs. Nothing is saved until you press Save.
