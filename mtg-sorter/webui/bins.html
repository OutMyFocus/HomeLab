What goes in each bin

Build a rule for anything: lands, tokens, a color, a price, the cards in your decks, extra copies, a format, words in the rules text. Rules are checked from the top and a card goes into the bin of the first rule it fits. Cards no rule fits (and cards it can't read) go to the catch-all bin. The counts show how your collection would be split, so you can try things before saving.
