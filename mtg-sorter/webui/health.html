      Bin sensors

      How to wire them

      Each TCRT5000 has 3 pins to use: VCC to the Pi's 3.3 V (pin 1 or 17, never 5 V), GND to the GND Wago, and DO to its bin's pin: bin 1 GPIO16 (pin 36), bin 2 GPIO20 (pin 38), bin 3 GPIO21 (pin 40), bin 4 GPIO12 (pin 32). Leave A0 empty. Then tick the switch above. Turn the little screw on a sensor until its second LED lights with cards stacked up to the slot and goes out with the bin empty.
