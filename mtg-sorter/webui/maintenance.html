Check-up

Restart and power

Restart the app if the page misbehaves. Reboot if the camera or Wi-Fi went missing. Always shut down before unplugging, so the SD card isn't damaged.

Updates

This copy runs as a Docker image on the server. Updates arrive as a new image: on Unraid's Docker tab press Check for updates, then Update next to mtg-sorter. Your cards and settings stay.

Card database

The scanner can only recognize cards it has a picture fingerprint for. Download every card gets all of them from Scryfall (about 108,000 printings, 30-60 minutes) and refreshes their prices. Scanning keeps working while it runs, and new cards are recognized as they arrive. Run it again after a new set comes out.

Test the moving parts

Moves every servo through its positions and back, then turns the feeder for a moment. Keep fingers and cards clear. To change the angles, use the Machine page.
