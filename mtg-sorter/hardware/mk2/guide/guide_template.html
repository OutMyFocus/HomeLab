{{TOC}}Wiring
Centre every servo before it goes in. Plug it into the PCA9685, set it to 90° with the snippet below, then fit the horn and slide the servo into its box. Otherwise the part it moves ends up at the wrong angle.
from adafruit_servokit import ServoKit
kit = ServoKit(channels=16)
kit.servo[0].angle = 90   # channel, angle
Never use a 12 V adapter. Mk2 runs on one 5 V brick and has no regulator, so 12 V would kill the Pi, the servos and the lights.

WIRINGPower and pins

One plug, one button

5 V 10 A brick ─ panel jack (back) ─┬─ Wago 5 V ─┬─ USB-C pigtail → Pi 5 (always fed)
                                    │            └─ relay COM ─ NO ─ Wago "switched 5 V"
                                    │                                 ├─ PCA9685 V+ (servos)
                                    │                                 ├─ DRV8833 VCC (feeder motor)
                                    │                                 ├─ light strip 5 V
                                    │                                 └─ button's LED ring
                                    └─ Wago GND ─ everything's ground
  • The power button is a 16 mm momentary button on the Pi 5's J2 pads (solder a 2-pin header: the only soldering in Mk2). Press to boot, press to shut down, hold to force off.
  • The relay is on GPIO22, with its trigger jumper on HIGH. Until the app starts, the servos, motor and lights have no power, so nothing twitches at boot.
  • Run sudo rpi-eeprom-config --edit on the Pi and add PSU_MAX_CURRENT=5000.
  • Servo plugs: brown is GND, orange is 5 V, yellow is signal.
